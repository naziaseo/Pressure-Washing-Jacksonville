Why Pool Decks Get Dirty in Jacksonville

Sun, Water and Bare Feet — Jacksonville Is Hard on Pool Decks

A Jacksonville pool deck is used most of the year. Every swim leaves something behind: sunscreen on the surface, pool chemicals splashed over the edge, and wet feet carrying dirt back onto the deck. Then the Florida sun bakes it in, and the next swim adds another layer.

Summer storms add their share too. Rain washes leaves, pollen and roof debris onto the deck, and it settles into paver joints and grout lines. Where the deck stays damp — in the shade, under trees, or by the screen enclosure — algae starts to grow, and that is the part that turns green and slippery underfoot.

That is why a quick rinse is rarely enough. A rinse moves loose dirt, but the staining that has soaked in, the film left by sunscreen, and the algae in the joints need a cleaning treatment and a method matched to the surface — concrete, pavers, tile and coping all clean differently.

Good to know: when you request a quote, tell us what your deck is made of and how long the stains have been there. Older, deeper marks may only fade, and knowing the material helps us choose the right approach before we start.

Four Signs to Look For

How to Tell Your Pool Deck Needs Cleaning

Walk your pool deck barefoot after the next rain. If any of these sound familiar, your deck is telling you something.

01

Green or Dark Patches

Green, grey or dark patches appear on the deck, especially in the shade. Algae and mildew have started to grow.

02

The Deck Feels Slippery When Wet

Wet feet slide on the surface instead of gripping it. Algae and organic buildup can make a wet deck slippery.

03

Stains That Will Not Rinse Off

Sunscreen, pool chemicals and weather stains stay put after rain or a rinse. They have soaked into the surface.

04

Dirt Stuck in Joints and Grout

Paver joints and grout lines look dark and full of dirt. Debris has settled in and a rinse cannot reach it.

Gentle on Pool Decks

Why High Pressure Is Wrong for Pool Decks

The Controlled Way

Pool decks are cleaned with a method matched to the material — concrete, pavers, tile and coping all clean differently. A cleaning treatment loosens dirt, algae and mildew first, then the surface is cleaned evenly so the finish stays consistent.

What High Pressure Can Do

High pressure can wash sand out of paver joints, chip tile and coping, and leave uneven marks on concrete. That is why we do not use it as the first answer on a pool deck.

  • Checked first, then cleaned
  • Method matched to the material
  • Joints, grout and coping considered
  • Rinsed and inspected at the end

Which method is right for your pool deck? There is no single method for every deck. The right approach depends on the material, the condition and the type of buildup. More pressure does not automatically mean better cleaning — on a pool deck, it usually means the opposite.

Pro Tip

Check the Shady Corner First

Pool Deck Cleaning FAQs

Common Questions

What pool deck materials can you clean?

Suitable concrete, pavers, and tile pool decks. We check the material and condition first.

Can pool deck cleaning make the deck less slippery?

Cleaning can remove algae and organic buildup that can make wet surfaces slippery. It does not change the texture of the deck material.

Do I need to drain the pool?

No. Pool deck cleaning is done around the pool. Keep the pool area clear of toys and furniture before we arrive.

How often should a pool deck be cleaned?

It depends on use, shade, nearby trees, and visible buildup. Many pool decks need cleaning at least once a year.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily, based on access. Tell us how to reach the pool deck and where the water supply is.

Will cleaning damage my pavers or joints?

We check the pavers and joints before cleaning and choose a method suited to the material and condition. High pressure is not used as the first answer on a pool deck, so sand is not washed out of the joints by default.

Can you clean the coping and walkways too?

Yes. Suitable pool coping and the walkways and seating areas around the pool can be cleaned together with the deck.
