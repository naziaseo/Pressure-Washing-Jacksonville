PROFESSIONAL HOUSE WASHING

Professional House Washing for Jacksonville Homes

Your home's exterior is exposed to Jacksonville's rain, humidity, sunlight, pollen, dirt, and everyday outdoor buildup. Over time, these conditions can leave siding, stucco, brick, and other exterior surfaces looking dull or discolored.

Our house washing service in Jacksonville, FL focuses on removing visible dirt, grime, algae, mildew, and organic buildup from suitable exterior surfaces. We use an appropriate cleaning approach based on the material and condition of your home rather than treating every surface the same way.

Whether your home needs a seasonal exterior refresh or has noticeable buildup that needs attention, professional house washing can help restore a cleaner, more maintained appearance.

A Cleaning Approach Based on Your Home's Exterior

Different exterior materials can respond differently to pressure and cleaning treatments. We assess the surface type, existing buildup, and overall condition before choosing a suitable approach. THE RIGHT METHOD MATTERS

Soft Washing vs. High-Pressure Washing

What Is Soft Washing?

Soft washing is a cleaning approach that uses lower water pressure together with an appropriate cleaning treatment. It can be considered for exterior surfaces where high pressure may be unnecessary or unsuitable.

What Is High-Pressure Washing?

High-pressure washing uses greater water pressure to remove buildup from suitable durable surfaces. It can be appropriate for certain exterior materials when used with proper control.

Which method is right for your home? There is no single cleaning method that is appropriate for every exterior surface. The right approach depends on the material, surface condition, type of buildup, and surrounding property conditions. Using more pressure does not automatically mean better cleaning.

BEFORE WE ARRIVE

What to Do Before House Washing

  • Move outdoor furniture away from areas that may be accessed during cleaning.
  • Remove toys, decorations, tools, planters, and other loose items near the home's exterior.
  • Keep exterior doors and windows closed while cleaning is underway.
  • Move or protect items that may be sensitive to water or cleaning treatments.
  • Mention existing stains, damaged surfaces, delicate landscaping, or other areas needing special consideration.

HOUSE WASHING FAQS

Common Questions

What is house washing?

House washing is the process of cleaning suitable exterior surfaces of a home to address visible dirt, grime, algae, mildew, pollen, and other outdoor buildup.

Is house washing the same as pressure washing?

Not always. House washing may involve controlled pressure washing or a lower-pressure soft washing approach depending on the surface material and condition.

Can house washing remove algae and mildew?

House washing can address visible algae and mildew on suitable exterior surfaces. Results may vary depending on the type, extent, and condition of the buildup.

Can you clean vinyl siding?

Yes, suitable vinyl siding can be cleaned using an approach appropriate for its condition and the type of buildup present.

Can you clean stucco and brick?

Suitable stucco and brick surfaces can be assessed for an appropriate cleaning method based on the material, condition, finish, and existing buildup.

How often should a house be washed?

There is no universal schedule. Moisture, shade, surrounding vegetation, weather exposure, and visible buildup can affect how often exterior cleaning may be needed.

Can house washing damage an exterior?

Improper pressure or cleaning methods can potentially affect some exterior surfaces. That is why the material and condition should be assessed first.

Do I need to be home during house washing?

Not necessarily, depending on the property and access arrangements. It is helpful to discuss access requirements before service begins.
