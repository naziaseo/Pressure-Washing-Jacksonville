The Right Method Matters

Soft Washing vs. High-Pressure Washing

What Is Soft Washing?

Soft washing uses lower water pressure together with the right cleaning treatment. It is the gentler choice for exterior surfaces where high pressure may be unnecessary or unsuitable.

What Is High-Pressure Washing?

High-pressure washing uses greater water pressure to remove buildup from suitable durable surfaces, with proper control.

  • Low pressure, like a garden hose
  • Kills growth at the root
  • Results that last longer
  • Plant-safe when done right

Which method is right for your home? There is no single method for every surface. The right approach depends on the material, the surface condition, the type of buildup and the property around it. More pressure does not automatically mean better cleaning.

Before We Arrive

What to Do Before House Washing

A few small steps before we arrive help the wash go smoothly.

  • Move outdoor furniture away from areas that may be accessed during cleaning.
  • Remove toys, decorations, tools, planters, and other loose items near the home’s exterior.
  • Keep exterior doors and windows closed while cleaning is underway.
  • Move or protect items that may be sensitive to water or cleaning treatments.
  • Mention existing stains, damaged surfaces, delicate landscaping, or other areas needing special consideration.

House Washing FAQs

Common Questions

What is house washing?

House washing is the process of cleaning suitable exterior surfaces of a home to address visible dirt, grime, algae, mildew, pollen, and other outdoor buildup.

Is house washing the same as pressure washing?

Not always. House washing may involve controlled pressure washing or a lower-pressure soft washing approach based on the surface material and condition.

Can house washing remove algae and mildew?

House washing can address visible algae and mildew on suitable exterior surfaces. Results may vary based on the type, extent, and condition of the buildup.

Can you clean vinyl siding?

Yes, suitable vinyl siding can be cleaned using an approach right for its condition and the type of buildup present.

Can you clean stucco and brick?

Suitable stucco and brick surfaces can be checked for the right cleaning method based on the material, condition, finish, and existing buildup.

How often should a house be washed?

There is no universal schedule. Moisture, shade, surrounding vegetation, weather exposure, and visible buildup can affect how often exterior cleaning may be needed.

Can house washing damage an exterior?

Improper pressure or cleaning methods may affect some exterior surfaces. That is why the material and condition should be checked first.

Do I need to be home during house washing?

Not necessarily, based on the property and access arrangements. It is helpful to discuss access requirements before service begins.
