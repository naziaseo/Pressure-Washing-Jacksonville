The Right Method Matters

Soft Washing vs. High-Pressure Washing

What Is Soft Washing?

Soft washing uses lower water pressure together with the right cleaning treatment. It is the gentler choice for exterior surfaces where high pressure may be unnecessary or unsuitable.

What Is High-Pressure Washing?

High-pressure washing uses greater water pressure to remove buildup from suitable durable surfaces, with proper control.

  • Low pressure, like a garden hose
  • Kills growth at the root
  • Results that last longer
  • Plant-safe when done right

Which method is right for your home? There is no single method for every surface. The right approach depends on the material, the surface condition, the type of buildup and the property around it. More pressure does not automatically mean better cleaning.

Your Lawn & Property

We Protect Your Lawn, Plants & Outdoor Furniture

Pressure washing uses water and cleaning treatments right next to your lawn, flower beds, patio furniture and outdoor electronics. Protecting them is part of the job, not an extra.

  • Lawn and plants near the house are rinsed with plain water before and after, so no cleaning solution is left sitting on them.
  • Delicate plants are covered before we start — point out anything you are worried about and we will cover it.
  • Patio furniture and movable items are moved out of the way and put back where they were when the job is done.
  • Outdoor electronics near the work area — such as ring cameras and doorbells — are pointed out and covered before cleaning starts.

Before We Arrive

What to Do Before We Arrive

A few small steps before we arrive help the wash go smoothly.

  • Move outdoor furniture away from areas that may be accessed during cleaning.
  • Remove toys, decorations, tools, planters, and other loose items near the home’s exterior.
  • Keep exterior doors and windows closed while cleaning is underway.
  • Move or protect items that may be sensitive to water or cleaning treatments.
  • Mention existing stains, damaged surfaces, delicate landscaping, or other areas needing special consideration.

My HOA sent me a letter — how fast can you clean?

Can you clean a new-build home’s driveway?

Yes. New-build driveways often carry construction dust and red clay stains. Our driveway and concrete cleaning removes that buildup — and a wash soon after move-in keeps it from setting in.

Do you seal pavers in St. Johns?

Yes. We clean and seal paver driveways, patios and pool decks in St. Johns County. Sealing helps new pavers hold their colour and keeps joint sand in place. See our paver cleaning and sealing page for how it works.

Will pressure washing damage my plants or lawn?

Cleaning solutions can burn grass and plants if they sit on foliage or pool on the lawn — so we pre-wet plants, cover delicate ones, control runoff, and rinse during and after the job. Read how we protect your lawn and plants for the full routine.
