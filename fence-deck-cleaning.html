Professional Fence & Deck Cleaning in Jacksonville

Fences and decks are exposed to Jacksonville's rain, humidity, sunlight, pollen, dirt, and everyday outdoor conditions. Over time, these surfaces can develop algae, mildew, organic buildup, discoloration, and weather-related residue.

Our fence and deck cleaning service in Jacksonville, FL focuses on cleaning suitable wood, vinyl, composite, and other appropriate outdoor surfaces according to their material and condition. We use a controlled cleaning approach rather than applying the same pressure to every surface.

Whether your fence needs a seasonal refresh or your deck has developed visible buildup, professional cleaning can help improve the appearance of your outdoor areas and keep your property looking more maintained.

BEFORE WE ARRIVE

What to Do Before Fence & Deck Cleaning

  • Move chairs, tables, grills, planters, and other furniture away from the cleaning area.
  • Remove decorations, toys, tools, outdoor equipment, and other loose items.
  • Keep nearby doors and windows closed while cleaning is underway.
  • Move or protect items that may be sensitive to water or cleaning treatments.
  • Mention damage, loose boards, delicate landscaping, painted areas, staining, or other conditions needing consideration.

FENCE & DECK CLEANING FAQS

Common Questions

Can you clean a wood fence?

Suitable wood fences can be professionally cleaned using a controlled method based on wood condition and type of buildup.

Can you clean a vinyl fence?

Yes, suitable vinyl fencing can be cleaned to address dirt, grime, algae, mildew, and outdoor residue.

Can pressure washing damage a wood deck?

Improper pressure or technique can potentially damage wood. The deck condition should be assessed before choosing the method.

Can you clean composite decking?

Suitable composite decking can be cleaned, but manufacturer care recommendations and material condition should be considered.

Will fence cleaning remove algae and mildew?

Professional cleaning can address visible algae and mildew on suitable fence materials. Results vary with the amount and condition of buildup.

Can cleaning prepare my deck for staining?

Cleaning can be part of preparation for staining on suitable wood surfaces. The deck should dry properly and be inspected before coating.

How often should a fence or deck be cleaned?

There is no universal schedule. Weather, shade, moisture, vegetation, material, and visible buildup can affect timing.

Will cleaning remove every stain?

No cleaning method can guarantee removal of every stain. Results depend on the type, age, depth, and condition of the stain.

Do I need to be home during fence or deck cleaning?

Not necessarily, depending on property and access arrangements. Access requirements can be discussed before service begins.
