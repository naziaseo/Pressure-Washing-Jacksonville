What’s on Your Windows?

Know the Buildup Before You Clean It

Jacksonville’s pollen, rain and humidity leave their mark on glass. Here is what we see most — and window cleaning can help remove it.

01

Pollen and dust settle on glass and frames, especially during Jacksonville pollen season, and leave a dull yellow-grey film.

02

Rain and sprinkler water can leave spots and mineral marks on exterior glass. Fresh spots usually clean off; older, deeper marks may only fade.

03

Algae & Mildew

Moisture and shade can bring algae and mildew around frames and sills. A cleaning treatment loosens them so they rinse off.

04

Cobwebs & Insects

Cobwebs and insects often collect around windows, screens and frames — and they show up fast on glass that was just cleaned.

Why Windows Go Dull in Jacksonville

It Is Not Just Dirt — It Is Jacksonville Weather

Jacksonville windows work hard. In pollen season, a fine yellow dust settles on glass within days. Summer rain dries on the glass and leaves spots. Sprinklers hit the same lower windows every morning and leave mineral marks that get harder to remove the longer they sit.

Shade and humidity add their own problem: algae and mildew grow around frames and sills, especially on the north side of a home or under big trees. Near the beaches, salt air leaves its own film. None of this means the glass is ruined — it means the buildup has had time to settle in.

That is also why a quick wipe rarely lasts. Glass can look clean for a day and dull again once the frames and screens around it are still dirty. Cleaning the glass, frames, sills and suitable screens together is what makes the result hold.

Good to know: if your windows face a sprinkler, tell us when you request a quote. Moving the sprinkler head, or cleaning those windows a little more often, keeps the spots from becoming the kind that only fade instead of coming off.

Four Signs to Look For

How to Tell Your Windows Need Cleaning

Stand inside on a bright day and look out. If any of these sound familiar, your windows are telling you something.

01

The Light Looks Grey

The room feels darker than it should, even with the curtains open. A film on the glass is cutting the light.

02

Spots After Rain

Every shower leaves spots and streaks behind. The glass is no longer shedding water cleanly.

03

Dusty Screens

Tap a screen and dust puffs out, or cobwebs hang in the corners. Dirty screens re-dirty clean glass fast.

04

Green Around the Frames

The frames and sills look green or dark in the corners. Moisture and shade have let growth settle in.

Gentle on Glass

Why High Pressure Is Wrong for Windows

The Gentle Way

Windows are cleaned with a gentle method and the right cleaning treatment — low pressure, careful wiping and a thorough rinse. Glass, seals and frames are not built to take a blast of pressure.

What High Pressure Can Do

High pressure can force water past seals, damage frames and screens, and leave glass worse than before. That is why we do not use it on windows.

  • Low pressure on glass and seals
  • Frames, sills and screens cleaned too
  • Checked first, then cleaned
  • Rinsed and inspected at the end

Which method is right for your windows? There is no single method for every window. The right approach depends on the glass, the frame material, the condition and the type of buildup. More pressure does not automatically mean better cleaning — on glass, it usually means the opposite.

Before We Arrive

What to Do Before Window Cleaning

A few small steps before we arrive help the cleaning go smoothly.

  • Move outdoor furniture and planters away from windows that will be cleaned.
  • Remove toys, decorations and other loose items near the windows.
  • Keep windows closed while cleaning is underway.
  • Move or protect items near windows that may be sensitive to water or cleaning treatments.
  • Mention cracked glass, loose screens, damaged frames, or other areas needing special consideration — and tell us how many windows you have when you request a quote.

Window Cleaning FAQs

Common Questions

What is included in window cleaning?

Exterior glass, frames, sills and suitable screens are included. Tell us how many windows you have when you request a quote.

Do you clean windows inside the home?

This service focuses on exterior windows. If you need interior cleaning, mention it when you request a quote and we will tell you what is possible.

Can you clean high windows?

Windows that can be reached safely from the ground or with suitable equipment can be cleaned. We check access before we start.

What about hard-water spots from sprinklers?

Fresh spots usually clean off. Older, deeper mineral marks may only fade, not disappear completely — results vary with the type, extent and condition of the buildup. Tell us about sprinkler spots when you request a quote.

Do you clean the screens as well?

Yes, suitable screens can be cleaned as part of the service. Dirty screens re-dirty clean glass quickly, so cleaning them together helps the result last.

How often should windows be cleaned?

There is no fixed schedule. Pollen, rain, shade, sprinklers and nearby trees can affect how often cleaning is needed.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily, based on access. Exterior windows can often be cleaned when you are away, if access and water supply are arranged.
