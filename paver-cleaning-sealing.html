Florida vs Your Pavers

Your Pavers Fight Florida Every Single Day

Florida sun fades paver colour. Rain and humidity feed algae in the joints. Leaves, pollen and tyre marks settle into every gap. Pavers do not wear out all at once — they fade a little, darken a little, and grow a little green, until one day the whole driveway looks tired.

The joints are the weakest link. Dirt, weeds and algae live there first, and joint sand is easy to disturb if the cleaning is too aggressive. That is why pavers and joints need careful attention — the material, the joint condition and the existing buildup are considered before any cleaning method is chosen.

The Longer You Wait, the Harder the Job

A light film washes off easily. Deep, settled buildup in the joints takes more work, and some old stains may only fade, not vanish completely. Cleaning sooner keeps the pavers brighter — and leaves them ready for sealing, if sealing suits them.

Good to know: sealing is not part of cleaning. It is a separate step, after cleaning, and only when the pavers are clean, dry and suitable for it.

Myth or Fact

Paver Sealing Facts

Three things people say about pavers. Tap a board to see if it is true.

Myth

Sealing needs pavers that are clean and dry first. A dirty or damp surface can affect the final result, so preparation and drying come before any sealer.

Myth

Too much pressure disturbs joint sand and can damage the surface. A controlled approach, matched to the pavers and the buildup, cleans without tearing the joints apart.

Myth

Sealing is a protective treatment, not a force field. Dirt, pollen and algae can still settle on sealed pavers — sealing helps the surface resist stains and makes cleaning easier, it does not stop buildup forever.

After Cleaning

Paver Sealing After Cleaning

Cleaning and sealing are two different stages of paver maintenance. Pavers should be properly cleaned and prepared before a sealer is applied.

01

What Is Paver Sealing?

Paver sealing applies an appropriate protective coating or treatment to suitable paver surfaces. The sealer depends on material, condition, location, and desired finish.

02

When Should Pavers Be Sealed?

Timing depends on paver material, existing sealer, surface condition, exposure, and maintenance history. Sealing may be considered after proper preparation.

03

Preparing Pavers Before Sealing

Pavers should be clean and appropriately prepared. Visible dirt, buildup, weeds, and other concerns should be addressed first.

04

Why Proper Application Matters

Sealer application requires suitable coverage, preparation, and drying conditions. A dirty or damp surface can affect the final result.

1

Clean

Paver Cleaning & Sealing FAQs

Common Questions

What is paver cleaning?

Paver cleaning removes visible dirt, grime, algae, mildew, pollen, organic debris, and other buildup from suitable paver surfaces.

Can you clean paver driveways?

Yes, suitable paver driveways can be cleaned according to their material, condition, and type of buildup.

Can you clean paver patios?

Yes, suitable paver patios can be cleaned to address visible dirt, algae, mildew, pollen, and organic buildup.

Can you clean paver joints?

Paver joints can be cleaned, but joint sand condition should be considered because excessive pressure can disturb it.

Will paver cleaning remove weeds?

Cleaning can address visible organic growth in joints, but recurring weeds may require additional maintenance or treatment.

What is paver sealing?

Paver sealing applies an appropriate sealer to prepared paver surfaces. The sealer depends on material, condition, location, and intended result.

Do pavers need to be cleaned before sealing?

Yes. Pavers should be properly cleaned and prepared so visible dirt, organic buildup, and other concerns are addressed first.

How often should pavers be sealed?

There is no universal schedule. Exposure, traffic, material, existing sealer, weather, and maintenance can affect resealing timing.

Can you seal pavers immediately after cleaning?

Sealing requires appropriate preparation and drying conditions. Pavers should be sufficiently clean and dry first.

Can damaged or loose pavers be repaired during cleaning?

Cleaning and repair are separate services. Damaged or loose pavers can be identified, but cleaning does not correct structural problems.

Will paver cleaning remove every stain?

No cleaning method can guarantee removal of every stain. Results depend on the type, age, depth, and condition of the stain.
