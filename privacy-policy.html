Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This page explains how Jacksonville Pressure Wash, run by Nazia Akbar ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you visit flpressurewashingjacksonville.com or contact us.

Information You Choose to Share

This website has no online form or account signup. If you email or call us, you choose what to share. This may include your name, contact details, property or service details, and your message. Please do not send sensitive information unless your request needs it.

Information Collected When You Visit

The service that hosts this website may collect basic technical information to run and protect the site. This may include your IP address, browser and device details, pages you visit, the page that referred you, and the date and time of your visit. The host may use server logs to keep the site working, fix problems, and improve security.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and service or estimate requests.
  • Communicate about requested services and related arrangements.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the website and our business.
  • Meet legal obligations and address disputes or misuse.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

This website does not use analytics or advertising scripts. The host may use tools needed to run and secure the site. The Manrope font is stored on this website. Photos load from Unsplash and Pexels. When your browser requests those photos, these services may receive technical information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle that information.

You can manage cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some tools may affect parts of this website. Other websites have their own privacy policies.

Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information. Service providers may handle information to run the website or communicate with you. We may also share it when the law requires or when needed to protect rights, safety, or security. We keep information only as long as needed for its purpose, our business needs, or legal requirements.

Security and Your Choices

We use reasonable steps to protect information. Still, no website or online message is fully secure. You can choose not to contact us. You can also ask us to correct or delete information you sent before. We will respond as the law requires, but may need to keep some information for business or legal reasons.

Children's Privacy

This website is for a general audience, not children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. We will post any update here with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Nazia Akbar at info@flpressurewashingjacksonville.com.
