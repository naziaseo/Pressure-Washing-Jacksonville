Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jacksonville Pressure Wash, operated by Nazia Akbar ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you visit flpressurewashingjacksonville.com or contact us about our services.

Information You Choose to Share

This website does not currently provide an online form or account registration. If you contact us by email or phone, you may choose to share information such as your name, contact details, property or service details, and the contents of your message. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Information Collected When You Visit

The service that hosts this website may automatically process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, referring page, and the date and time of a visit. Hosting providers may use server logs for reliability, troubleshooting, and security.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and service or estimate requests.
  • Communicate about requested services and related arrangements.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the website and our business.
  • Meet legal obligations and address disputes or misuse.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

This website does not currently include analytics or advertising scripts. The website host may use essential technologies to deliver or secure the site. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash; those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests their files. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and practices.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect how parts of this website work. Links to other websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that help operate the website or communicate with you, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security. We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was provided, ongoing business needs, and applicable legal requirements.

Security and Your Choices

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. You may choose not to contact us or may ask us to correct or delete information you previously sent. We will consider and respond to requests as required by applicable law, subject to information we need to retain for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Nazia Akbar at info@flpressurewashingjacksonville.com.
