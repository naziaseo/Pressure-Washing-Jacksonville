The Right Method Matters

Soft Washing vs. High-Pressure Washing

What Is Soft Washing?

Soft washing uses lower water pressure together with the right cleaning treatment. It is the gentler choice for exterior surfaces where high pressure may be unnecessary or unsuitable.

What Is High-Pressure Washing?

High-pressure washing uses greater water pressure to remove buildup from suitable durable surfaces, with proper control.

  • Low pressure, like a garden hose
  • Kills growth at the root
  • Results that last longer
  • Plant-safe when done right

Which method is right for your home? There is no single method for every surface. The right approach depends on the material, the surface condition, the type of buildup and the property around it. More pressure does not automatically mean better cleaning.

Pro Tip

Wash Windows After the House Wash

In Atlantic Beach, salt spray settles on clean glass fast. Wash the windows after the house wash — not before — so the house wash does not leave spray on glass you just cleaned. And rinse the pool deck after beach days: sand left sitting on the deck grinds in and stains over time.

Before We Arrive

What to Do Before Pressure Washing

A few small steps before we arrive help the wash go smoothly.

  • Move outdoor furniture away from areas that may be accessed during cleaning.
  • Remove toys, decorations, tools, planters, and other loose items near the home’s exterior.

Common Questions

Do you wash beach cottages in Atlantic Beach?

Yes. We wash beach cottages, oceanfront condos, single-family homes, townhomes and rental properties across Atlantic Beach — house washing, window cleaning and roof cleaning, with the method matched to each surface.

Does salt air damage my home’s exterior?

Salt air leaves a hazy film on siding, windows and trim, and over time that buildup can dull paint and glass. Regular washing removes the film before it settles in. We check the surface first and choose a gentle approach suited to coastal homes.

Can you clean my windows after the house wash?

Yes — and that is the best order. We wash the house first, then clean the windows, so salt spray from the house wash does not settle on glass that is already clean.

Do you clean roofs near the beach?

Yes. We clean roofs in Atlantic Beach with low-pressure soft washing, which lifts black streaks and algae gently without harsh pressure on shingles. Coastal roofs pick up algae fast in the humidity, so regular roof cleaning helps them look their best.
